Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Yaro Technology LLC ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you play Bus Jam Rush: Parking Puzzle (the "Game") on iOS or Android. By using the Game you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Game.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

  • Game progress stored on your device. Your level, coins, boosters and settings (sound, vibration) are saved locally on your phone. This data is not sent to us and is deleted when you uninstall the Game.
  • Information collected by advertising partners. The Game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may automatically collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), IP address, approximate (coarse) location, device model and operating system, app interactions with ads, and diagnostic data.
  • Purchases. If you buy "Remove Ads", the payment is handled entirely by Apple through your App Store account. We never see your card or payment details; we only receive a confirmation that the purchase was made, which is stored on your device so ads stay off. You can restore it anytime with Settings → Restore Purchases.
  • If you contact us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and use it only to reply to you.

2. How information is used

  • To run the Game and remember your progress on your device.
  • To show ads that keep the Game free, measure ad performance and prevent ad fraud.
  • To answer your support requests.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Advertising, tracking and your choices

  • iOS: the Game asks for your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before your advertising identifier can be used for personalized ads. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. You can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device's Google settings.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: we use Google's consent tool to ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice anytime in the Game under Settings → Ad Privacy Choices.

Learn how Google uses data: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Third-party services

The Game uses Google AdMob (Google LLC) and its ad partners to show ads. These services act under their own privacy policies. We do not control the information they collect.

5. Data sharing

We do not share personal information with anyone except the advertising partners described above, or when required by law, to protect our rights, or with your consent.

6. Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the Game. We do not keep any game data on our servers. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you. To ask about or delete any information connected to you, email us at the address below.

7. Children's privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. To use these rights, email us. We will respond within the time required by law.

We take reasonable measures to protect information. However, no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.
