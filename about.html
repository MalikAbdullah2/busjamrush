About the Game

Bus Jam Rush: Parking Puzzle is a colorful traffic-jam puzzle. Tap cars, minibuses and buses to drive them out of a packed parking lot, park them in the bus bays, and get every passenger on the bus that matches their color. Plan your moves, because a blocked bay can end the round.

🚌Hundreds of levelsPuzzles that start easy and get trickier, with cars, minibuses and big buses.
⏱️Time RushClear wave after wave before the clock runs out.
📅Daily ChallengeA fresh puzzle every day.
🚁BoostersShuffle passengers or lift a stuck bus out with the helicopter.
